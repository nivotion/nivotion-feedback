# Feedback privacy

Issues and Discussions in this community repository are public. Treat everything you post as visible to other people.

Do not upload private or sensitive CSV contents. If data is needed, create a tiny synthetic CSV that reproduces the issue. Redact names, emails, customer data, local paths, tokens and private business data from screenshots, logs and attachments.

Security vulnerabilities belong in the product repository's private vulnerability reporting mechanism, never in public Issues or Discussions. If the private route is unavailable, wait until it is enabled; do not post the details publicly.

The static feedback page includes no analytics, external trackers, third-party font/script imports, forms that upload data, cookies added by NIVOTION or browser storage. Its buttons navigate to GitHub when configured. GitHub's own hosting and account policies apply when you visit its services; this page does not claim GitHub itself collects no information.
