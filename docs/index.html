BEFORE YOU SHARE

Keep your data private.

Do not upload private or sensitive CSV contents. If reproduction data is useful, create a tiny synthetic CSV instead.

Redact names, emails, customer data, local paths, tokens and private business data from screenshots and logs. Issues and Discussions are public.

Čeština je vítaná. Pomůže nám každý konkrétní postřeh. Neposílejte soukromá CSV ani citlivé údaje.

Security concerns need a private channel.

Use the product repository's private vulnerability reporting, once enabled. Never post vulnerability details in public Issues or Discussions. If the private route is unavailable, wait until it is enabled.
